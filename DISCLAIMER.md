# Disclaimer

OpenECRS is a research and evaluation project, not investment, legal, or business advice.

- **Evidence**: the evidence base is a fixed snapshot (2026-11-20) built from public sources. Labels come from weak supervision, not full human annotation; confidences are rule-based and uncalibrated.
- **Web search**: optional SearXNG (self-hosted) plus Bing fallback. Web pages are external and not part of the audited evidence base. Respect each upstream search engine's robots/terms; SearXNG is intended for local, low-volume use, not commercial high-concurrency service.
- **Browser fallback**: Playwright MCP is optional; respect target-site rules and do not scrape aggressively.
- **Uploads**: files and OCR/RAG artifacts stay local under `./uploads`; you are responsible for the privacy and rights of uploaded content. Do not expose this app to untrusted networks.
- **Estimates**: PoC effort/cost and web snippets are heuristic; validate before making decisions.
